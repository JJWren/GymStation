Rules

  • Never restripe. Two stripes is the mark, not a rank claim — it does not change with a member's belt.
  • Never tenant-color the bar or stripes. Only the field takes --tenant; the bar stays --belt so the mark can't be confused with a real belt bar.
  • No outline stroke on filled versions, no shadow, no rotation. The patch sits square; stamps rotate, marks don't.
  • Minimum size 16px square (favicon) / 20px in UI. Below that use the wordmark alone.
