GymStation Design System

ACADEMY LEDGER

The professor's paper systems — attendance cards, the dues book, the belt roster — made digital.
THE SIGNATURE — the belt bar. Rank + stripes as a true-color physical strip anchoring every profile, roster card, and promotion timeline. Belt colors are never tenant-themed.

Direction decisions

  • D1 — Academy Ledger. Card/patch surfaces, stamped statuses ([PAID], [DUE], [COVERED—ANA]), sturdy grotesque display + mono data type. One scoreboard moment reserved for the instructor live-roll.
  • D2 — Role-adaptive shell. Admin: left rail as ledger index-tabs. Member: top bar on desktop, bottom tab bar on mobile (Schedule · Check-in · Diary · Progress).
  • D3 — Schedule. Desktop: 7-column week grid, edit-in-place, substitution stamps. Mobile: today-centered day list with filter chips and window-aware check-in.
  • D4 — Both modes, dark is base. Dark = mat surface / gi-fabric weave; cards are stitched patches. Light = the paper ledger, first-class. WCAG-validated tenant tokens in both.
  • D5 — Public hero. Tenant mat photo + crest + configurable CTA, then a live "This week" strip from real schedule data.

Standing principles

  • Platform owns structure, typography, behavior. Tenant owns accent color, logo, imagery.
  • Belt colors are sacred — always true to rank, in both modes, under every tenant theme.
  • Semantic tokens only; no raw hex in components.
  • Mono type = data (counts, dates, money, stamps, eyebrows). Display face is rationed.
